LittleLog

A private baby tracker — diapers, feeds, sleep, medications & milestones.

What LittleLog is

LittleLog is a mobile app (Android package com.rango.baby_tracker) that helps parents and caregivers track a baby's daily life. It is local-first: everything you log is stored on your own device. There is no account and no LittleLog server.

What you can do

  • Daily logs — diapers, bottle & breast feeds, sleep, solids, temperature, and your own custom categories, with optional notes and photos.
  • Medications — schedule doses and get reliable on-time alarms; track given/missed/skipped doses.
  • Milestones — a month-by-month photo grid for baby's first year.
  • Insights — daily summaries and trend charts.
  • Multiple babies — switch between profiles.

Why LittleLog requests Google account access

Cloud backup is optional. If you choose to enable it, LittleLog asks you to sign in with Google and requests a single, narrow permission:

Google Drive — app data (drive.appdata) scope

This scope grants access only to a hidden, app-private folder in your own Google Drive ("Application Data folder"). LittleLog uses it solely to store a backup snapshot of your LittleLog data (babies, logs, medications, milestones, photos) so you can recover it after reinstalling the app or moving to a new phone.

What we explicitly do not do

  • We do not read, list, modify, or delete any of your other Google Drive files — the drive.appdata scope cannot see them.
  • We do not send your data to any LittleLog or third-party server. The backup goes directly from your device to your own Google Drive.
  • We do not use your Google profile, contacts, or email for advertising or profiling.

You can turn automatic backup off, delete all backups from within the app, or revoke access at any time at your Google Account permissions.
