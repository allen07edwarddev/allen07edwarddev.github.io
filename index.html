What it does

Everything in one gentle place

LittleLog (Android package com.rango.baby_tracker) helps parents and caregivers track a baby's day without the noise. There is no LittleLog account and no LittleLog server — your data lives on your phone.

Daily logs

Diapers, bottle & breast feeds, sleep, solids, temperature, and your own custom categories — with notes and photos.

Medication alarms

Schedule doses and get reliable on-time alarms. Track given, missed and skipped doses at a glance.

Milestones

A month-by-month photo grid for baby's first year, ready to look back on and share.

Insights

Daily summaries and trend charts so patterns in feeding and sleep are easy to see. Switch between multiple babies.

Your data & Google access

Transparent by design

Cloud backup is optional. If you turn it on, LittleLog asks to sign in with Google and requests one narrow permission — nothing more.

Google Drive · app-data scope (drive.appdata)

This grants access only to a hidden, app-private folder in your own Google Drive. LittleLog uses it solely to store a backup snapshot of your data so you can recover it after reinstalling or moving phones.

What LittleLog does

  • Stores your backup directly in your Google Drive's hidden app folder — over an encrypted connection.
  • Keeps only the 5 most recent snapshots; older ones are removed automatically.
  • Lets you turn backup off, clear all backups in-app, or revoke access anytime from your Google Account.

What LittleLog never does

  • Read, list, or modify any of your other Google Drive files — the scope can't even see them.
  • Send your data to a LittleLog or third-party server. We have no server and never receive a copy.
  • Use your Google profile, contacts or email for advertising or profiling.

LittleLog's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
