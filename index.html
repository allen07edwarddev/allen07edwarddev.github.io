Everything a busy household needs.

Designed around how families actually shop — split between people, scattered across stores, scribbled on the back of a receipt at midnight. Grozi pulls it all together.

🛒

Shared lists, in real time

Add an item from the bus, watch it appear on your partner's phone before they reach the dairy aisle. No "did you get it?" texts.

✨

AI that knows groceries

Describe a meal — "Spaghetti carbonara for 4" — and Grozi builds the list, in your local currency, with price estimates and friendly thumbnails.

📍

Group by store, or by aisle

Switch between by-store and by-category views so the list matches how you actually walk the shop.

⏰

Schedule reminders

Set a weekly Saturday-morning ping, or a one-time nudge before the party. Reminders fire on every household member's phone.

💸

Budget at a glance

See what you've spent vs. what's pending. Let the AI estimate a budget from your list if you don't have one yet.

📦

Reuse last week's run

Old lists archive cleanly. One tap to duplicate them as a fresh active trip — your weekly staples never get retyped.
