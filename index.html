Rango Tech is an independent software studio — one person designing, building and maintaining every product end to end.

The work is deliberately small and opinionated: privacy and longevity over growth metrics. No analytics SDKs, no behavioral tracking, nothing sold. Apps work offline; the only network use is what you explicitly opt into, such as your own cloud backup.
