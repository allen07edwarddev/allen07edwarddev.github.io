house rules ↴

Principles that don't bend.

Three rules govern every product — and none of them move for growth metrics.

1

Private by design

Your data stays on your device, or is shared only with the people you invite. No tracking SDKs, no ads, no behavioral analytics — nothing collected, nothing sold.

2

Calm by default

Each app does one job well and then gets out of the way. No engagement loops, no streaks, no notifications competing for your attention.

3

Yours to keep

Export and back up your data anytime — including to your own cloud storage. If an app ever shuts down, your records leave with you.
