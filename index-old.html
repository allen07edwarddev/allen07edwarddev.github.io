§01 — Principles
001
Private by design
Your data stays on your device, or is shared only with the people you invite. No tracking SDKs, no ads, no behavioral analytics — nothing collected, nothing sold.
002
Calm by default
Each app does one job well and then gets out of the way. No engagement loops, no streaks, no notifications competing for your attention.
003
Yours to keep
Export and back up your data anytime — including to your own cloud storage. If an app ever shuts down, your records leave with you.

Rango Tech is an independent software studio — one person designing, building and maintaining every product end to end.

The work is deliberately small and opinionated: privacy and longevity over growth metrics. No analytics SDKs, no behavioral tracking, nothing sold. When an app connects to the network — syncing a shared list, hosting a karaoke room, backing up to your cloud — it does only that, and only because you asked.
